Learn from demonstration. Vision Pro feeds the data; Diffusion Policy and ACT turn it into skills; the controller deploys them — stitched into long-horizon autonomy, onboard.

Curate the data in Data Curation.

A dataset-quality curation studio for MABEL imitation-learning data. It ingests recorded episodes, automatically flags the defects that poison a policy — dropped frames, out-of-sync streams, frequency drops, dead motors, missing video — grades every episode, and lets you align, blade, trim, and cull non-destructively in a Final-Cut-style timeline before exporting a clean dataset in LeRobot format. The Rerun-style 3D viewer and seven synced camera feeds are still there to review every frame.

Data engine

Every demo is a label.

Collection is not a separate chore — it is a side effect of using the robot. Teleoperate to do the task; the dataset writes itself.

Kinesthetic / teleop data capture
Capture

From the headset to HDF5.

Each Vision Pro session streams synchronized head and wrist images, full joint states, executed actions, and the goal image to disk. Episodes are stored as HDF5 — one file each, one schema shared by sim and the real robot — with dataset statistics computed for normalization at train time. Full data infrastructure →

Policies

Two families, one interface.

Both consume the same observation graph and emit action chunks, so swapping a policy is a checkpoint change, not a rewrite.

Diffusion Policy schematic
Diffusion Policy

Multi-modal, smooth, robust.

A conditional denoising policy models the full distribution of expert actions, so MABEL can commit to one of several valid strategies instead of averaging them into failure. Receding-horizon action chunks keep motion smooth and reactive — the primary model for contact-rich bimanual skills.

Action chunkingVisual conditioningReceding horizon
ACT / transformer schematic
ACT

Transformers that plan ahead.

Action Chunking with Transformers predicts a horizon of future actions from a short observation window, with temporal ensembling for stability. It is a strong, fast baseline and a natural fit for the bimanual demonstrations the teleop rig produces.

CVAE optionTemporal ensembleGoal image input
Training

Reproducible by construction.

Fixed seeds, bundled statistics, and auto-resume. Re-run the same command and continue from the last checkpoint.

01
Sim pre-train, real fine-tune

Policies are warm-started in the Isaac Lab twin, then fine-tuned on real teleoperation episodes — fewer real demonstrations for the same competence.

02
Normalization travels with the model

Dataset statistics are saved alongside each checkpoint and applied at inference to un-normalize actions, so a model behaves identically on the bench and on the robot.

03
Ablations, not anecdotes

RGB-only, +goal-image, and +joint-history variants are trained and evaluated under one harness, so every design choice is backed by a measured number.

04
Onboard deployment

Checkpoints run on the Jetson Thor through the policy runtime, handing actions to the whole-body controller — no laptop in the loop.

Long-horizon

Laundry. From 28 to 6.

One task, twelve sub-steps, two elevators, three doors, and one floor of a Manhattan high-rise that didn't know a robot was coming. Each step is a learned skill or a navigation goal; a task graph stitches them end-to-end and runs autonomously.

Long-horizon rollout · drop demo reel
Step 01 · 00:00

Picks up the laundry bag.

Bimanual lift from the bedroom floor. The policy chooses the grasp; the second hand stabilizes the bag's weight transfer onto the lift column.

Navigates to the elevator.

SLAM-based global plan; the holonomic base strafes around the kitchen island, holding heading so the wrist cameras keep the bag in view.
