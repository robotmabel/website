04 · Teleop

Wear the goggles. Be the robot.

A visionOS app turns Apple Vision Pro into MABEL's senses. Hand, finger, and gaze stream to the robot at sub-100 ms — and every session writes itself into the training set.

Teleoperation

You move. It moves.

No joystick, no keyboard. Pinch to grasp, raise your arm to lift, look where you want MABEL to look — and every session auto-logs into the learning pipeline.

Vision Pro POV · live stream
visionOS app

Embodiment, not remote control.

The operator sees MABEL's stereo feed in the headset and reaches into the scene. Twenty-six tracked hand joints map onto each 17-DOF ORCA hand; head pose drives the 3-DOF neck; gaze cues the active-perception system. The result feels less like driving a robot and more like wearing one.

01
Hand-to-hand retargeting

visionOS hand tracking is solved per finger onto the ORCA kinematics, preserving contact intent rather than raw joint angles — a pinch stays a pinch even though human and robot hands differ.

02
Sub-100 ms loop

WebRTC over local Wi-Fi 6 keeps perception-to-actuation latency low enough for embodied immersion; the operator stops thinking about lag and starts thinking about the task.

03
Data collection by default

Every demonstration doubles as a labeled trajectory — images, joint states, actions, and goals — written to HDF5 and compatible with ALOHA/ACT and Diffusion Policy out of the box.

Intent in, motion out

Safe to stand beside

Compliant, gravity-compensated arms mean a missed grasp bumps — it doesn't break. Teleoperate next to the robot, not behind glass.
