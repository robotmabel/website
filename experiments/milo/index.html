Method notes

Palm position is forward kinematics of the measured encoder angles on MILO's URDF, torso included, neck level. It excludes backlash and link bending, so every number here is a lower bound on the true end-effector error.

Gains. The experiments use test-only stiff gains (repeatability: arms kp ×8, torso ×5; path following: ×6 / ×4), restored to the compliant tuned set on exit. Every commanded line was checked sample by sample for self-collision, tip-over and joint limits before anything moved; no guard tripped.

The 3D replays pose MILO's rig (ORCA v1 hands) from the logged joint angles. Joints that were not logged are drawn at their default home pose: hands open, neck level, lift down.

Error budget. Palm deviation is linearised per station, Δp ≈ J(q̄)Δq; the residual of that approximation is mm at the worst station. Cross-terms between joints are ignored, so shares do not add exactly to RP.
