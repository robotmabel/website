Abstract

A whole-body robot you can actually build.

A research-grade mobile manipulator, mostly 3D-printed. Two 7-DOF arms, two 17-DOF ORCA hands, a holonomic base, an active head — 51 joints in all. One Jetson Thor runs everything, untethered. Vision Pro teleop doubles as the data engine. All of it open.

Open hardware, sub-$10k

3D-printed, off-the-shelf, twin 17-DOF hands. Full BOM and CAD released.

Whole-body control

Base to fingertips, one system. The body reaches — not just the arm.

Vision Pro teleop

Hand and gaze at sub-100 ms. Every session trains the robot.

Long-horizon autonomy

Learned skills stitched to navigation. Multi-step, onboard.

In the wild

MABEL in New York.

We took the robot outside — across bridges, through plazas, around tourists. The platform that handles a Manhattan high-rise should also handle a Manhattan sidewalk. Crowds, curbs, glass doors, and uneven pavement are not edge cases here; they're the test set.
