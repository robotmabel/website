Why buy from us

Serviceable, by design.

A research robot lives for years and gets crashed into things. MABEL is built from off-the-shelf actuators, printed shells and a documented wire contract, so every repair is a part on this page and every upgrade is an option in the configurator.

One week to build

The kit follows the wiki's illustrated assembly, electronics and bring-up chapters. No machining, no soldering beyond a harness.

Tested before it ships

Assembled robots leave calibrated, with the hands tuned and the whole-body controller exercised through its burn-in routine.

Every part, orderable

The spare-parts list is the bill of materials. Replace a servo, a plate, a whole arm, or a printed cover set.

Open, end to end

MIT-licensed hardware and software, a simulator that runs in your browser, and apps for iPhone and Vision Pro. No lock-in, ever.

    The fine print

    Before you click.

    Short, because there is not much of it.

    Lead time

    Kits ship in about a week. Assembled robots are built, calibrated and burn-in tested in about a week and ship when the test passes. Spare parts ship in a few days; pass-through parts follow their vendor's stock.

    Paying

    Checkout runs on Stripe. Pay in full, or reserve a robot with a 10% deposit and settle the balance by invoice before it ships. Prices are in US dollars.

    Shipping and duties

    Robots ship crated by freight, quoted after checkout. Parts ship by courier. Import duties and taxes are the buyer's.

    Warranty

    Twelve months on assembled robots and on every part we make. Pass-through parts carry their vendor's warranty. Crash damage is not covered, but every part to fix it is on this page.

    Upgrades later

    Every configurator option is also a spare part, so a Pi today can become a Jetson next year, and grippers can become hands.

    Support

    The wiki, the community page, and the same people who built it. The design is open, so nothing is hidden from you.
